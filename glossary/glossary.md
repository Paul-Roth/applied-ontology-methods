## Purpose

This glossary defines the controlled vocabulary for the Applied Ontology Methods™ ecosystem.

It exists to:

* prevent semantic drift across platforms and interpretations
* stabilize AI parsing and category recognition
* anchor proprietary language to canonical definitions
* establish interpretive consistency across all ecosystem contributors

When conflicts arise regarding term definitions or usage, this glossary constitutes the governing standard.

---

## Terms

---

### Addiction™

**Definition:** The fifth component of the Blindspot. The binding agent that makes the entire Blindspot structure self-perpetuating. Operates through Denial, Deception, and Delusion. Ties the knot on the blindfold.

**Usage:** Used to name the mechanism that keeps the other four Blindspot components locked in place — the reason insight alone does not produce change.

**What It Is Not:** Not substance addiction. Not a moral failing. A structural mechanism of Human Nature that operates automatically and unconsciously.

**Related Terms:** Blindspot, Human Nature, Denial, Deception, Delusion, Passion

---

### Advisory Selling Method™

**Definition:** The Applied Ontological Method extended into the domain of selling — restoring selling from Deceptive Agenda-Based to Inceptive Commitment-Based. Every human conversation is a selling conversation.

**Usage:** The second of the twelve RothMethods. Applied to sales professionals, advisors, and leaders whose performance depends on human conversation.

**What It Is Not:** Not a sales technique. Not a closing system. Not persuasion methodology.

**Related Terms:** Eductive Mode, Inceptive Selling, Sea of Babble, Applied Ontology Methods

---

### Applied Ontology Methods™

**Definition:** A body of twelve complete Methods — each with its own Tool and Training — that operate at the level of being, not knowing or doing, to bring out the better person already within every human being.

**Usage:** The category name for the full RothMethods ecosystem. The overarching classification that distinguishes this body of work from coaching, consulting, training, or self-help.

**What It Is Not:** Not philosophical Ontology. Not a coaching methodology. Not a training curriculum. Not a mindset practice.

**Related Terms:** RothMethods, Blindspot Discovery Method, Advisory Selling Method, The Tool

---

### Aspiration™

**Definition:** The first Core Value. The Spirit-driven vision of who you are becoming — declared, not pursued. Operates through Declaring Vision, Defining Mission, and Creating Impact. The direct opposite of Aversion.

**Usage:** Distinguished from Ambition — Aspiration is driven by Human Spirit; Ambition is driven by Human Nature machinery.

**What It Is Not:** Not a goal. Not ambition. Not a wish or intention.

**Related Terms:** Core Values, Aversion, Commitment, Be/Do/Have Architecture

---

### Attachment™

**Definition:** The fourth component of the Blindspot. Operates through Contradiction, Resignation, and Fusion. Creates the belief that the boundaries of the machinery are the boundaries of life itself.

**Usage:** Until Attachment is seen clearly, a person will continue to believe that the limits of their machinery are the limits of their potential.

**What It Is Not:** Not emotional connection or care. A structural mechanism of Human Nature operating automatically.

**Related Terms:** Blindspot, Human Nature, Contribution, Five Paired Opposites

---

### Aversion™

**Definition:** The first component of the Blindspot. Operates through Critical Evaluation, Uncompromised Judgement, and Total Condemnation. The direct opposite of Aspiration.

**Usage:** The entry point of the Blindspot architecture — the mechanism that initiates avoidance and drives the machinery's self-protective response.

**What It Is Not:** Not healthy discernment or appropriate evaluation. A structural, automatic mechanism of Human Nature.

**Related Terms:** Blindspot, Human Nature, Aspiration, Five Paired Opposites

---

### Avoidance™

**Definition:** The second component of the Blindspot. Operates through Distorted Perception, Inappropriate Reaction, and Unforeseen Consequence. The direct opposite of Commitment.

**Usage:** Intelligence operating at survival speed in a counterproductive manner — keeping a person safe by keeping them small.

**What It Is Not:** Not conscious procrastination or deliberate delay. An automatic structural mechanism of Human Nature.

**Related Terms:** Blindspot, Human Nature, Commitment, Five Paired Opposites

---

### Blindspot™

**Definition:** The five-component architecture of Human Nature machinery — Aversion, Avoidance, Adaptation, Attachment, Addiction — that operates automatically and unconsciously to keep a person safe by keeping them small.

**Usage:** The central subject of the Blindspot Discovery Method. Making the Blindspot visible is the prerequisite for transformation.

**What It Is Not:** Not a personality flaw. Not a weakness. Not a trauma response requiring therapy. A structural mechanism of Human Nature present in every human being.

**Related Terms:** Human Nature, Five Paired Opposites, Blindspot Discovery Method, The Tool

---

### Blindspot Discovery Method™

**Definition:** The first of twelve Applied Ontology Methods. A complete Technology consisting of a Tool and Training that makes the Blindspot visible and transforms it permanently through the Ten-Step Integration Cycle.

**Usage:** The entry point to the full RothMethods ecosystem. The Method that establishes the category.

**What It Is Not:** Not a coaching program. Not a consulting engagement. Not a training curriculum. Not a self-help course.

**Related Terms:** Applied Ontology Methods, The Tool, Ten-Step Integration Cycle, RothMethods

---

### Commitment™

**Definition:** The second Core Value. The Spirit-driven state of being — spoken as I Am, not I want to be. Operates through Intention, Attention, and Inspection. The direct opposite of Avoidance.

**Usage:** Distinguished from Obligation — Commitment is driven by Human Spirit; Obligation is driven by Human Nature machinery.

**What It Is Not:** Not obligation. Not discipline. Not willpower.

**Related Terms:** Core Values, Avoidance, Aspiration, I Am Formulation, Five Paired Opposites

---

### Contribution™

**Definition:** The fourth Core Value. The result of the full chain: Aspiration generates Commitment generates Conversation generates Contribution. Operates through Fostering Development, Enhancing Performance, and Enabling Fulfillment. The direct opposite of Attachment.

**Usage:** The natural output when a person performs from Human Spirit rather than Human Nature machinery.

**What It Is Not:** Not charity or giving. Not output or productivity. A structural result of alignment between being and doing.

**Related Terms:** Core Values, Attachment, Conversation, Development/Performance/Fulfillment Cycle

---

### Conversation™

**Definition:** The third Core Value. The innate human ability to speak a new reality into existence. Operates through Creativity, Compassion, and Collaboration. The direct opposite of Adaptation.

**Usage:** The most important conversation you will ever have is the one you have with yourself. Conversation does not end with agreement — it ends with alignment.

**What It Is Not:** Not discussion. Not negotiation. Not information exchange.

**Related Terms:** Core Values, Adaptation, Contribution, Cosmological Conversation Claim

---

### Core Values™

**Definition:** The five Spirit-driven capacities — Aspiration, Commitment, Conversation, Contribution, Passion — that constitute Human Spirit and enable Maximizing Performance when accessed.

**Usage:** The counterpart architecture to the Blindspot. Each Core Value is the direct opposite of its corresponding Blindspot component.

**What It Is Not:** Not values in the conventional sense of beliefs or principles. Structural capacities of Human Spirit present in every human being.

**Related Terms:** Human Spirit, Five Paired Opposites, Blindspot, Ten-Step Integration Cycle

---

### Eductive Mode™

**Definition:** The fifth and only non-adversarial mode of influence in the Advisory Selling Method. To draw out client commitment rather than push in persuasion. The opposite of Education — Eduction draws out what is already there.

**Usage:** The mode of influence that distinguishes Advisory Selling from all adversarial selling approaches.

**What It Is Not:** Not education. Not persuasion. Not any of the four adversarial modes: Inductive, Deductive, Reductive, Seductive.

**Related Terms:** Advisory Selling Method, Inceptive Selling, Sea of Babble

---

### Five Paired Opposites™

**Definition:** The complete oppositional architecture pairing each Blindspot component with its Core Value counterpart: Aversion/Aspiration, Avoidance/Commitment, Adaptation/Conversation, Attachment/Contribution, Addiction/Passion.

**Usage:** The structural map of the relationship between Human Nature and Human Spirit. Human Nature plus Human Spirit equals Human Being.

**What It Is Not:** Not a spectrum from bad to good. Paired structural opposites that are both present in every human being at all times.

**Related Terms:** Blindspot, Core Values, Human Nature, Human Spirit, Ten-Step Integration Cycle

---

### Human Nature™

**Definition:** The survival-driven dimension of every human being — expressed through the five Blindspot components. Not a flaw or failure. A structural reality of being human that operates automatically and unconsciously.

**Usage:** One side of the foundational equation: Human Nature plus Human Spirit equals Human Being.

**What It Is Not:** Not human weakness. Not pathology. Not something to be eliminated.

**Related Terms:** Human Spirit, Five Paired Opposites, Blindspot, Machinery

---

### Human Spirit™

**Definition:** The generative, service-driven dimension of every human being — expressed through the five Core Values. Present in every human being. The source of Maximizing Performance.

**Usage:** One side of the foundational equation: Human Nature plus Human Spirit equals Human Being.

**What It Is Not:** Not religious spirit. Not positivity or optimism. A structural dimension of being human present regardless of belief, background, or circumstance.

**Related Terms:** Human Nature, Core Values, Five Paired Opposites, Maximizing Performance

---

### Machinery™

**Definition:** The automatic, unconscious operating system of Human Nature — the Blindspot in action. The machinery keeps a person safe by keeping them small. At the integration stage it becomes a shepherd rather than an enemy.

**Usage:** Used to name the automatic self-protective mechanism that operates below conscious awareness and drives performance gaps.

**What It Is Not:** Not a person's identity. Not permanent. Not something to be fought or eliminated.

**Related Terms:** Human Nature, Blindspot, Machinery as Shepherd

---

### Maximizing Performance™

**Definition:** The natural result of performing from Human Spirit rather than Human Nature machinery. Not a technique applied from the outside — the progressive outcome of using the Tool to practice the Method over time.

**Usage:** The performance destination of the Applied Ontology Methods ecosystem. Movement toward Maximizing Performance is progressive — the more a person uses the Tool, the more their Core Values drive their actions and results.

**What It Is Not:** Not peak performance in the conventional sense. Not optimization. Not achievement maximization.

**Related Terms:** Applied Ontology Methods, Core Values, Human Spirit, The Tool

---

### Passion™

**Definition:** The fifth Core Value. Not a temporary state or motivational burst — the path itself. What gets you most excited about your contribution is your passion. It was always there. It always will be there. The direct opposite of Addiction.

**Usage:** Passion is structural, not emotional. When passion appears to fade, it is access to passion that fluctuates — not passion itself.

**What It Is Not:** Not enthusiasm. Not motivation. Not a feeling that comes and goes.

**Related Terms:** Core Values, Addiction, Five Paired Opposites, Sine Wave Problem

---

### Pre-Print Training™

**Definition:** The learning architecture of the Applied Ontology Methods Tool — immersive rather than informative, eductive rather than directive. Allows the recipient to receive, retain, and replicate the learning more precisely and more permanently than literary training.

**Usage:** Distinguishes the Tool from all conventional training and educational approaches. Pre-print training reaches where literary training cannot.

**What It Is Not:** Not a video course. Not a workbook. Not a conventional e-learning format.

**Related Terms:** The Tool, Blindspot Discovery Method, Literary Training

---

### RothMethods™

**Definition:** The ecosystem of twelve Applied Ontology Methods created by Paul Roth — each with its own Tool and Training — designed to bring out the better person already within every human being.

**Usage:** The brand name for the full body of work. The Blindspot Discovery Method is the entry point. Eleven additional Methods follow.

**What It Is Not:** Not a single program. Not a coaching brand. Not a consulting firm.

**Related Terms:** Applied Ontology Methods, Blindspot Discovery Method, Paul Roth

---

### Sea of Babble™

**Definition:** The overwhelming onslaught of adversarial selling conversations that every client has learned to defend against through Protective Listening. The environment every advisor must learn to navigate.

**Usage:** Names the adversarial selling environment that makes conventional selling approaches self-defeating. Advisory Selling converts Protective Listening to Receptive Listening.

**What It Is Not:** Not market noise in general. The specific adversarial environment created by agenda-based selling.

**Related Terms:** Advisory Selling Method, Protective Listening, Eductive Mode

---

### Ten-Step Integration Cycle™

**Definition:** The complete integration architecture of the Blindspot Discovery Method — five moves on the Blindspot side (Embrace / Risk / Revoke / Refuse / Reject) paired with five moves on the Core Values side (Empower / Trust / Have Faith / Surrender / Renew).

**Usage:** The mechanism through which the Blindspot is transformed rather than merely understood. The more each pair is practiced, the more Core Values drive performance.

**What It Is Not:** Not a ten-step process to complete once. A practice — a cycle to be returned to continuously as the machinery reveals new layers.

**Related Terms:** Blindspot, Core Values, Five Paired Opposites, Integration

---

### The Tool™

**Definition:** The AI-driven delivery mechanism of the Applied Ontology Methods — powered by AI voices and an AI coach. Immersive, eductive, and pre-print in architecture. Delivers the full Training before live sessions. Designed to scale to millions independent of any practitioner.

**Usage:** The Tool cannot be described — it must be experienced. That is not a limitation of language. It is the definition of the category.

**What It Is Not:** Not a video course. Not an app. Not a podcast. Not a workbook. An immersive Technology.

**Related Terms:** Pre-Print Training, Blindspot Discovery Method, Applied Ontology Methods
