## Governance Principles

The Applied Ontology Methods™ ecosystem operates according to the following principles:

* The problem is ontological — it lives at the level of Being, not knowing or doing
* The better person is already within every human being
* Human Nature plus Human Spirit equals Human Being
* Transformation is something becoming more true to itself
* The Tool cannot be described — it must be experienced from within
* Terminology must maintain semantic consistency across all platforms and expressions
