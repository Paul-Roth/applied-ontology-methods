# Proof Record — Cosmetics Company

category: "applied-ontology-methods"
updated: "2025"

## Context

A cosmetics company with an established sales force operating toward an annual revenue target. The engagement involved developing and deploying the Advisory Selling Method™ under significant time pressure.

## Starting Constraint

The company had a $30 million annual revenue goal. Standard sales approaches were in place but not producing the results the organization required. The ceiling was structural — rooted in how the sales force was conducting selling conversations.

## Applied Ontology Methods™ Application

The Advisory Selling Method™ — developed overnight for this specific engagement. Inceptive, not Deceptive. Commitment-based, not agenda-based. The Eductive Mode deployed in place of adversarial influence strategies.

## Implementation Summary

The Advisory Selling Method™ was developed and deployed rapidly — demonstrating the method's capacity to produce results under real-world conditions without extended implementation timelines. The sales force shifted from adversarial selling strategies to the Advisory approach.

## Outcome

The $30 million goal was exceeded. The company delivered $34 million.
