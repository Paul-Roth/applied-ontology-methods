# Blindspot Discovery Method™

## The Applied Ontological Technology of Transformation

The Blindspot Discovery Method™ is the first of twelve Applied Ontology Methods™ — a complete Technology consisting of a Tool and Training that makes the Blindspot visible and transforms it permanently through the Ten-Step Integration Cycle.

It is based on a single governing law:

> The Blindspot cannot be overcome by effort, discipline, insight, or conventional training.
> It can only be seen — and seeing it, precisely and completely, is what transforms it.
> The Tool makes that seeing possible for the first time.

---

# Core Function

The Blindspot Discovery Method™ exists to solve a single systemic problem:

> Accomplished people have a gap between who they see they could be and how they perform. That gap does not respond to conventional intervention — because its source is ontological, not informational.

Rather than adding new knowledge or skills on top of existing machinery, the Blindspot Discovery Method™ replaces the informational approach with an ontological one — operating at the level of being, not knowing or doing.

---

# System Design Principles

The Blindspot Discovery Method™ operates according to five governing principles.

## 1. Applied

It works. It produces measurable, observable, permanent change in how people perform. Not theoretical. Not philosophical. Applied.

## 2. Ontological

It operates at the level of being — who a person is, not what they know or what they do. The Blindspot lives at the ontological level. Only a Technology that operates at that level can make it visible.

## 3. Method

It is precise, repeatable, and teachable. It has architecture. It has mechanics. It delivers. The same result is produced across every person who uses the Tool and practices the Method.

## 4. Eductive

The Tool draws out — it does not push in. The participant does the work. The Tool equips them to do it from within rather than delivering content to the mind from without.

## 5. Progressive

Movement toward Maximizing Performance is not instantaneous. The more a person uses the Tool to practice the Method, the more the machinery loses its power and the more their Core Values drive their actions and results.

# Structural Architecture

The Blindspot Discovery Method™ operates through two inseparable components:

| Component | Function |
|-----------|----------|
| The Tool™ | AI-driven immersive delivery mechanism — makes the Blindspot visible at the ontological level |
| The Training™ | Ten-lesson architecture — Diagnostic Arc and Integration Arc |

### The Tool™

* AI-driven — powered by AI voices and an AI coach
* Immersive, not informative. Eductive, not directive.
* Pre-print architecture — allows the recipient to receive, retain, and replicate the learning more precisely and more permanently than literary training
* Delivers the full Training before any live session
* Equips participants to do the deep inner work themselves
* Designed to scale to millions independent of any practitioner

*The Tool cannot be described. It must be experienced. That is not a limitation of language — it is the definition of the category.*

### The Training™

Ten-lesson architecture in two arcs:

**Diagnostic Arc — Lessons 1–5**
Makes the Blindspot visible across all five components:
* Lesson 1: Aversion
* Lesson 2: Avoidance
* Lesson 3: Adaptation
* Lesson 4: Attachment
* Lesson 5: Addiction

**Integration Arc — Lessons 6–10**
Transforms the Blindspot through the Ten-Step Integration Cycle:
* Lesson 6: Introduction to Integration
* Lesson 7: Commitment
* Lesson 8: Conversation
* Lesson 9: Contribution
* Lesson 10: Passion

# The Ten-Step Integration Cycle

The mechanism through which the Blindspot is transformed rather than merely understood:

* The more I embrace my Aversion, the more I can empower my Aspiration.
* The more I risk my Avoidance, the more I can trust my Commitment.
* The more I revoke my Adaptation, the more I can have faith in my Conversation.
* The more I refuse my Attachment, the more I can surrender to my Contribution.
* The more I reject my Addiction, the more I can renew my Passion.

This is not a process to complete once. It is a practice — a cycle to be returned to continuously as the machinery reveals new layers.
