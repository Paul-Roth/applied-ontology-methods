# The Core Values™

## Overview

The Core Values are the five-component architecture of Human Spirit — the Aspiration, Commitment, Conversation, Contribution, and Passion that are always present, always accessible, and always pointing toward who a person truly is.

They are not acquired. They are not developed. They are not earned through effort or discipline.

They were always there. The work is restoring access to them — not finding them for the first time.

---

## Why It Matters

Most performance systems treat values as aspirational targets — things to move toward, embody, or install. The RothMethods™ framework inverts this entirely.

The Core Values are not aspirational. They are structural. They exist in every human being as the architecture of Human Spirit. The machinery — the Blindspot — does not destroy them. It blocks access to them.

This distinction changes everything about how transformation works. The person is not being built into something new. They are being returned to what was always true.

---

## Core Definition

The Core Values are the five-component architecture of Human Spirit — the permanent, structural expression of who a person truly is, independent of what the machinery has done with that truth.

> *"Human Nature plus Human Spirit equals Human Being."*

## The Five Core Values

### Aspiration

The first Core Value. The vision, mission, and impact that express who a person truly is — not what the machinery has decided is safe or realistic.

**Three Dynamics:**
- Declaring Vision
- Defining Mission
- Creating Impact

**The Be / Do / Have Architecture:**
- Vision = Be
- Mission = Do
- Impact = Have

> *"That emptiness isn't because you chose the wrong goal. It's because you were following your machinery instead of your Aspiration."*

**Aspiration vs. Ambition:**
Aspiration is Spirit-driven. Ambition is machinery-driven. Ambition chases outcomes the machinery has decided will finally be enough. Aspiration points toward who a person is — not what they can acquire.

Aspiration is paired with Aversion. The more the Aversion is embraced, the more Aspiration can be empowered.

---

### Commitment

The second Core Value. Not obligation. Not resolution. Not willpower.

**Three Dynamics:**
- Intention
- Attention
- Inspection

> *"Obligation is what your machinery uses to control you. Commitment is how your Human Spirit guides you."*

**The I Am Formulation:**
Commitment is spoken as *I am* — not *I want to be*, not *I hope to be*, not *I am trying to be*. The grammar of Commitment is present tense. It closes the gap between being and doing.

**Commitment as recovery:**
You do not find your Commitment. You recover it. It was always there. The work is unearthing what you have already been committed to — often despite yourself.

**The Exit Framework:**
The machinery produces reasonable-sounding exits from Commitment. They do not feel like avoidance. They feel like wisdom, discernment, or timing. The practice of Commitment is recognizing the exit for what it is.

Commitment is paired with Avoidance. The more Avoidance is risked, the more Commitment can be trusted.

---

### Conversation

The third Core Value. The innate ability to speak a new reality into existence — not to report on existing reality, not to defend position, but to invent something from nothing as if it already exists.

**Three Dynamics:**
- Creativity — generating something new; *"creating from nothing something that already exists"*
- Compassion — *"the combination of deep understanding of people's situation and total intolerance for them not having what they deserve"*
- Collaboration — creating working together rather than against

> *"Our fate is not so much in our hands as it is in our mouths."*

> *"The most important conversation you will ever have is the one you have with yourself."*

**The Cosmological Claim:**
*"Our human world, which has been imposed on the natural world, would in no way exist unless it was first spoken into existence."*

Conversation is not communication. Communication reports. Conversation creates. The distinction is the difference between describing what is and declaring what will be.

Conversation is paired with Adaptation. The more Adaptation is revoked, the more faith can be placed in Conversation.

---

### Contribution

The fourth Core Value. The result of the full chain — Aspiration generates Commitment, Commitment generates Conversation, Conversation generates Contribution.

**Three Dynamics:**
- Fostering Development
- Enhancing Performance
- Enabling Fulfillment

**The Development → Performance → Fulfillment Cycle:**
The cycle runs in both directions. Development feeds performance. Performance feeds fulfillment. Fulfillment feeds development. This is not a linear progression — it is a self-reinforcing engine.

> *"Fulfillment is a job. It ain't hanging out. It's got to be on the schedule."*

**The Howard Paradox:**
Howard, a commercial real estate broker earning $500K annually, was told to pursue sailing 1000%. That year he made $2.4M. *"If he was 1,000% in sailing, he could be 1,000% when he was in brokerage."*

Fulfillment amplifies performance. This is not inspiration — it is architecture. The Development / Performance / Fulfillment cycle is bidirectional and structural.

Contribution is paired with Attachment. The more Attachment is refused, the more surrender to Contribution becomes possible.

---

### Passion

The fifth Core Value. Not enthusiasm. Not motivation. Not a temporary state.

**Three Dynamics:**
- What have I accomplished?
- What do I need to do?
- What's possible if I do that?

> *"Passion is the path."*

> *"What gets you most excited about your contribution is the passion, and it's the path."*

> *"You're not going to find a new one all of a sudden. It's always going to be there. And it's either you access it or you don't."*

**The Sine Wave Problem:**
When Passion appears to come and go, it is not Passion fluctuating. It is access to Passion fluctuating. The machinery cuts access. The work is restoring access — not finding new Passion.

**The Physiological Grounding:**
The heart has its own intelligence. "Follow your heart" is not sentiment. It is where Passion lives physiologically. Passion is structural, sustainable, and permanent — because it has been there since the beginning and will be there at the end.

Passion is paired with Addiction. The more Addiction is rejected, the more Passion can be renewed.

## The Five Paired Opposites

The Core Values do not stand alone. Each is paired with its corresponding Blindspot component — the Human Nature architecture that blocks access to it.

| Human Nature (Blindspot) | Human Spirit (Core Values) |
|--------------------------|---------------------------|
| Aversion | Aspiration |
| Avoidance | Commitment |
| Adaptation | Conversation |
| Attachment | Contribution |
| Addiction | Passion |

> *"Human Nature plus Human Spirit equals Human Being."*

The pairing is not incidental. It is structural. Each Blindspot component blocks access to its corresponding Core Value — and working with the Blindspot directly restores access to the Core Value it has been obscuring.

## Common Misunderstandings

**"These sound like values I need to develop."**
The Core Values are not developmental targets. They are structural realities. They are already present. The work is not development — it is restoration of access.

**"I already know what I'm passionate about."**
Knowing and accessing are not the same. The machinery can block access to Passion while leaving the conceptual knowledge of what Passion is fully intact.

**"This is about having better values."**
Performance Blindspot Transformation is not a values clarification exercise. The Core Values are the architecture of Human Spirit — not aspirational qualities to be improved upon. They are what is already true.
